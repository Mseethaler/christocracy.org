## Species: fields

Each field inherits its domain's four cells. Only the differentiation is shown: a government whose relation changes from the genus (in bold), or a note that sharpens the genus.

### 1. Worship

Divided by: Element of worship (WCF 21).

| Field | Summary | Differentiation |
|---|---|---|
| Lord's Day | One day in seven kept holy; first day of the week under the new Covenant | Civil: Ought to protect a public day of rest; (WCF 21.8, 23.3). |
| Oaths, vows, fasts, thanksgivings | Occasional elements of worship | Family: Household vows and fasts under the head (Num 30).<br>**Civil: P** (genus R). Administers lawful oaths in courts and offices (WCF 22.3); proclaims fasts and thanksgivings. |
| Prayer and thanksgiving | Required of all | Civil: Ought to proclaim or promote public days of prayer and thanksgiving. |
| Preaching and hearing | Ministers preach; all hear | Self: Hears with diligence and receives the Word (WLC 160); does not preach unless called and ordained or training or under supervision.<br>Family: The head brings the household to hear and reviews the sermon at home. <br>**Civil: N** (genus R). No authority over the content of the pulpit, unless heresy or grevious error. |
| Singing of psalms | God's praise sung in all facets of worship | Family: Household singing in family worship. |
| Sacraments | Administered by the church alone | Self: Receives worthily after self-examination (1 Cor 11:28).<br>**Family: N** (genus P). Presents children for baptism or trains in the faith and baptises on profession.<br>**Civil: N** (genus R). No authority over admission or administration. |
| Reading of Scripture | The Word read in closet, home and assembly | None |

### 2. Education

Divided by: Stage and kind of transmission.

| Field | Summary | Differentiation |
|---|---|---|
| Adult instruction | Lifelong discipleship of men and women | **Family: C** (genus P+C). Adult children leave parental direction (Gen 2:24); families still sponsor and encourage.Church: Discipleship and training of men and women. Refutes blasphemy and heresy |
| Apprenticeship and vocational training | A trade handed down from master to apprentice | Family: Fathers train sons in the household trade or place them with masters. Civil: Enforces contracts; no licensing beyond what justice requires. |
| Catechesis | The faith taught by question and answer | Church: works alongside fathers; ministers and elders examine. |
| Early nurture | The first years, in the home | Family: The mother's primary work in the earliest years, under the father's headship.**Church: R** (genus P+R). Supports and equips parents; no normative instructional role. |
| Elementary schooling | The grammar stage | None yet |
| Secondary schooling | The logic and rhetoric stages | None yet |

### 3. Economy

Divided by: Kind of productive work.

| Field | Summary | Differentiation |
|---|---|---|
| Agriculture and land | Tilling and keeping the ground | Family: Household farms; land held as inheritance across generations.Civil: Protects boundaries and enforces just weights; no subsidies or production planning. |
| Building trades | Houses and structures built soundly | Civil: Liability for negligent building (Deut 22:8, Ex 21:33-34); no open-ended code bureaucracy. |
| Commerce | Buying and selling justly | Civil: Just weights and measures: the core civil duty in this field. |
| Crafts and manufacturing | Making goods with skill | None |
| Finance and capital | Lending, saving and investing | Church: Teaches the ethics of lending, debt and surety (Ex 22:25, Prov 22:7, Prov 6:1-5). Civil: Honest money (Lev 19:35-36 applied to currency) and contract enforcement. Securities and insurance law apply to capital and risk pools. |
| Professions | Law, medicine, accounting, engineering | Church: Disciplines members for professional fraud and malpractice. Civil: Licensing: Distinguish punishing fraud from gatekeeping. |
| Technology | Tools and systems; information processing | None yet |

### 4. Mercy

Divided by: Object of need (Mt 25:35-36, Jas 1:27).

| Field | Summary | Differentiation |
|---|---|---|
| Disaster relief | Aid across congregations in calamity | Church: Inter-church collections, as for Jerusalem. **Civil: R** (genus N). Keeps public order during disaster; no general r>elief mandate, but promotes mercy in prudence, not coercion. |
| Orphans and adoption | Placing the fatherless in families | Family: Adoption and fostering are household work. **Civil: R** (genus N). Courts recognize guardianship and adoption and protect children from harm. |
| The poor and hungry | Restore to productive work | Church: The diaconate distinguishes need from idleness (1 Tim 5, 2 Thess 3:10). |
| Prisoners | Remember those in bonds | Church: Visitation and preaching in prisons. **Civil: P** (genus N). Holds custody. Biblical penology favors restitution and vengence over long imprisonment. |
| The sick and disabled | Prayer, provision and care | Family: Household care first. Church: Elders pray and anoint (Jas 5:14); deacons provide. Supported also by vocational medical professionals and risk pools |
| Strangers and hospitality | Welcome to the sojourner, reject invaders and heretics (Isa 10, 2 John) | **Civil: R** (genus N). Equal justice for the sojourner (Ex 12:49, Deut 1:16). |
| The unborn and mothers | Care for mothers; protection for children | Family: Kin support the mother. **Civil: P** (genus N). Protecting the unborn from murder is a duty of justice, not mercy; enforce retribution for sexual crimes in accordance with Biblical Law. |
| Widows and the elderly | Family first, then the church | Family: Children and grandchildren bear the first duty (1 Tim 5:4, 8). Church: Enrolled widows: those truly alone and of godly character (1 Tim 5:5, 9). |

### 6. Public order

Divided by: Function of justice (level of government is an attribute, as opposed to a species).

| Field | Summary | Differentiation |
|---|---|---|
| Arbitration | Private courts among believers | **Self: C** (genus P+C). Submits disputes to arbitration by compact. Family: Heads of households enter arbitration compacts. **Church: P+R** (genus R). Hears disputes between members. **Civil: R** (genus P). Enforces arbitration agreements. |
| Courts | Judging justly | Self: Jury service; honest witness. Church: Judges disputes between believers (1 Cor 6). |
| Defense | Protecting the people from attack | Self: Defense of household and neighbor. Family: Training sons; defending the home. |
| Legislation | Declaring and applying God's law and it's general equity | Self: Citizens petition and elect legislators. Church: Preaches the standard; does not legislate. Civil: Declares and applies God's law. |
| Policing | Restraining evildoers | Self: Defense of household and neighbor. Family: Defense of the home (Ex 22:2). |
| Political participation | Household voting, office and civic action | Self: Votes*, seeks office, petitions. Family: Heads of households as the base of the electorate; training sons. Church: Prays, preaches and disciplines and disciples officeholders.  **Civil: R** (genus P). Conducts elections honestly. |
| Restitution and punishment | Just sanctions for crime | Church: Discipline for sin; not civil penalty. Civil: Restitution and vengence. |
