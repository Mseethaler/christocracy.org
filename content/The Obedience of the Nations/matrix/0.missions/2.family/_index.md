**Family in Missions**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Household witness; support those sent |

## End

Bring the household and its neighbors under the Word, and support those the church sends.

## Means

**Direct**

- Gathering kin and neighbors to hear (Acts 10:24)
- Hospitality to unbelievers
- Raising children who witness

**Instrumental**

- Missionary support
- Sending sons
- Mission societies

## Agent

Head of household

## Norm

- Acts 10:24
- Acts 16:31-34
- Josh 24:15
- 3 Jn 5-8

## Boundary

Does not administer the sacraments or ordain.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Households with no witness to their neighbors; missions treated as a line in the church budget.

**Usurpation:** The household standing in for the church.

## Commentary

Covenant children are not objects of missions but of catechesis, which belongs to Education.
