**Church in Missions**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P (Principal) | Send, preach, baptize, plant |

## End

Gather the elect from the nations by the Word, the sacraments, and the planting of churches (WCF 25.3).

## Means

**Direct**

- Ordaining and sending missionaries
- Preaching to the unconverted
- Baptizing and receiving members
- Planting churches with elders

**Instrumental**

- Mission boards
- Presbytery mission committees
- Cooperative sending agencies

## Agent

Ministers, elders and presbyteries

## Norm

- Mt 28:18-20
- Rom 10:14-15
- Acts 13:1-3
- Acts 14:23
- WCF 25.3

## Boundary

Missions issue from churches with elders, not independent works accountable no church.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Churches failing to fund and send missionaries.

**Usurpation:** Missionaries operating outside of church oversight or accountability.

## Commentary

Parachurch mission agencies belong only as instruments, with the church as principal.
