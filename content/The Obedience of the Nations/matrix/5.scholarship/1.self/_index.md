**Self in Scholarship**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Think God's thoughts after Him; seek out what is concelaed; excell in vocation |

## End

Pursue understanding in a calling of study, taking every thought captive.

## Means

**Direct**

- Study
- Writing
- Disputation

**Instrumental**

- Faculty and fellows
- Learned societies

## Agent

The scholar

## Norm

- Prov 25:2
- 2 Cor 10:5
- Acts 17:11

## Boundary

The scholar remains under Scripture, his church and his household duties.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Anti-intellectualism.

**Usurpation:** Autonomous reason; the pride of knowledge (1 Cor 8:1).

## Commentary

Love the Lord with all your mind.
