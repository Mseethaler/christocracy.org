**Church in Scholarship**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+R (Principal + Regulative) | Guard doctrine; train ministers; refute error |

## End

Guard doctrine, train ministers, and test scholarship by Scripture.

## Means

**Direct**

- Confessional standards
- Ordination examinations

**Instrumental**

- Seminaries under church oversight

## Agent

Presbyteries and elders

## Norm

- 2 Tim 2:2
- Titus 1:9
- 1 Tim 3:15

## Boundary

Does not dictate method in disciplines beyond what Scripture teaches.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Seminaries independent of any church, drifting doctrinally.

**Usurpation:** Clerical control of all inquiry.

## Commentary

The Church should be directly and indirectly instrumental in the defense and
pursuit of higher learning, specifically with regard to the deeper understanding
and defense of the faith.
