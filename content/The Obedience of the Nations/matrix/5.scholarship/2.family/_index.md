**Family in Scholarship**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| C (Constitutive) | Raise, fund and send scholars |

## End

Raise and fund scholars; keep a household culture of learning.

## Means

**Direct**

- Household libraries
- Patronage

**Instrumental**

- Colleges
- Research institutes
- Think tanks
- Journals

## Agent

Head of household and members

## Norm

- Ps 78:5-7
- 2 Tim 1:5

## Boundary

Directs an academy through its charter and trustees, not by interfering in its daily rule.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Sons sent into debt for credentials from hostile institutions.

**Usurpation:** College treated as a status purchase.

## Commentary

Families ought to support and pursue higher learning as time and gifts allow.
