**Family in Economy**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Household dominion and inheritance |

## End

Household dominion: property, provision and inheritance across generations.

## Means

**Direct**

- Household enterprise
- Apprenticing children
- Property ownership
- Inheritance planning

**Instrumental**

- Guilds
- Christian business networks
- Cooperatives
- Farms
- Capital pools
- Revolving loan funds
- Land and equipment trusts
- Mutual assurance and risk pools

## Agent

Head of household

## Norm

- Gen 1:28
- Prov 13:22
- 1 Tim 5:8
- Prov 31:10-31
- Deut 8:18

## Boundary

Household enterprise must deal justly with employees' own households.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Consumer households with no productive property and no inheritance.

**Usurpation:** Exploiting neighbors; favoring kin over justice.

## Commentary

Legal check: risk pools touch insurance law and capital pools touch securities law. Clear both before launch.
