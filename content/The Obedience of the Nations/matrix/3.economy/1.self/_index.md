**Self in Economy**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Productive Labor in a vocation |

## End

Faithful labor in a calling, so as to provide and to have something to give.

## Means

**Direct**

- Work
- Skill
- Honesty
- Saving
- Avoiding debt

**Instrumental**

- Guild membership
- Apprenticeship

## Agent

The worker

## Norm

- Gen 2:15
- Eph 4:28
- 2 Thess 3:10
- Col 3:23
- Prov 22:29

## Boundary

A calling does not override duties to family or the Lord's Day.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Sloth, debt, envy.

**Usurpation:** Work as identity or idol; greed.
