**Civil in Economy**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| R (Regulative) | Enforce just weights and measures, contracts, restitution |

## End

Justice in exchange: just weights and measures, enforced contracts, punished theft and fraud, protected property.

## Means

**Direct**

- Courts
- Restitution

**Instrumental**

None.

## Agent

Magistrates and judges

## Norm

- Lev 19:35-36
- Deut 25:13-16
- Ex 22:1-15
- 1 Kgs 21

## Boundary

No authority to redistribute, plan production, debase currency or license trades beyond justice.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Failing to punish fraud.

**Usurpation:** Licensing regimes, currency debasement (dishonest weights), confiscatory taxation (1 Sam 8:15-17).
