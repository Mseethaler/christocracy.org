**Church in Economy**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| R (Regulative) | Teach the ethics of labor; discipline fraud |

## End

Teach the ethics of labor, property, wages, debt and lending; settle disputes and discipline fraud among members.

## Means

**Direct**

- Preaching
- Counsel
- Discipline
- Hearing disputes between believers

**Instrumental**

None.

## Agent

Elders

## Norm

- Lev 19:13
- Jas 5:4
- Ex 22:25
- 1 Cor 6:1-8

## Boundary

Does not run businesses, set prices or act as an employment bureau.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Silence on work and money; the prosperity gospel.

**Usurpation:** Church-owned commerce; membership used as a marketplace.

## Commentary

This row keeps a guild from becoming a mere trade union.
