**Self in Worship**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P (Principal) | Secret worship and Lord's Day keeping |

## End

Personal communion with God and growth in holiness.

## Means

**Direct**

- Secret prayer
- Scripture reading and meditation
- Self-examination
- Keeping the Lord's Day

**Instrumental**

None.

## Agent

The believer

## Norm

- Mt 6:6
- Ps 1:1-2
- Ex 20:8-11
- WCF 21.6

## Boundary

Private worship does not replace public worship, and may not invent forms God has not commanded.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Prayerlessness; the Lord's Day treated as leisure.

**Usurpation:** Individualism that sets private devotion against the church; self-made religion (Col 2:23).

## Commentary

The root of every other cell. A man who does not worship in secret will not order his household, vocation or vote to Christ.
