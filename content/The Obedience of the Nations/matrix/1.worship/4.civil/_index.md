**Civil in Worship**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| R (Regulative) | Protect and nourish the church; promote the true religion |

## End

Protect the church's liberty and public peace; restrain public crimes against the first table as God's law defines them.

## Means

**Direct**

- Protection of lawful assemblies
- Restraint of public blasphemy (contested in scope)

**Instrumental**

None.

## Agent

Magistrates

## Norm

- Isa 49:23
- Rom 13:3-4
- WCF 23.3 (1646 and 1788 texts differ)

## Boundary

May not administer Word or sacraments, exercise the keys, or compel faith.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Treating Christ as one option among many; failing to protect worship.

**Usurpation:** Erastianism: licensing churches, regulating doctrine or worship.

## Commentary

Open question: the scope of the magistrate's duty toward the first table. The original WCF 23.3 and the American revision answer differently; settle which position this map holds.
