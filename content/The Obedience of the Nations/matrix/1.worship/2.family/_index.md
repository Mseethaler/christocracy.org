**Family in Worship**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P (Principal) | Household worship led by the head |

## End

Sanctify the home and raise children in the fear of God.

## Means

**Direct**

- Daily family worship: reading, prayer, singing
- Ordering the household's Lord's Day
- Preparing the household for public worship

**Instrumental**

None.

## Agent

Father as head, with the mother

## Norm

- Deut 6:6-7
- Josh 24:15
- Eph 6:4
- WCF 21.6

## Boundary

Does not administer the sacraments or exercise church discipline.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** No family worship; spiritual formation outsourced to youth ministry.

**Usurpation:** Home-church separatism that replaces the instituted church.

## Commentary

The household altar is the first place worship becomes culture.
