**Church in Worship**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P (Principal) | Public worship in the means of grace |

## End

The public worship of God and the ministry of the means of grace.

## Means

**Direct**

- Preaching of the Word
- Administration of the sacraments
- Public prayer and psalmody
- Discipline

**Instrumental**

- Inter-congregational fellowship
- Pastors' fellowships
- Shared conferences

## Agent

Ministers and elders

## Norm

- Acts 2:42
- Heb 10:25
- Mt 28:19-20
- WCF 21, 27-30

## Boundary

Worship is regulated by Scripture; the church may not bind conscience beyond the Word or take up the sword.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Worship as entertainment; discipline abandoned.

**Usurpation:** Binding conscience by tradition; seeking civil power for the church as church.

## Commentary

The source of the river (Ezek 47). Everything downstream depends on faithful pulpits and sacraments.
