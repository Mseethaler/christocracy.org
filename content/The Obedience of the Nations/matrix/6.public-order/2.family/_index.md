**Family in Public order**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| C (Constitutive) | Households are the base of the polity |

## End

Form the polity from households: train sons for office and defend the home.

## Means

**Direct**

- Training sons for public duty

**Instrumental**

- Men's groups
- Legal defense associations
- Christian arbitration panels
- Policy associations

## Agent

Head of household

## Norm

- Deut 1:13
- Ps 127:5

## Boundary

The family does not execute civil justice.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Households with no public presence.

**Usurpation:** Clan rule; dynastic politics.

## Commentary

Reformation by discipleship: the placing of righteous men and policies, not a parallel state.
