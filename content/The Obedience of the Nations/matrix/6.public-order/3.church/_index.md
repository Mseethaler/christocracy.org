**Church in Public order**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| R (Regulative) | Prophetic witness; pray for rulers; discipline members |

## End

Preach God's law to rulers and ruled, pray for magistrates, and discipline members who hold office.

## Means

**Direct**

- Preaching
- Prayer for rulers
- Discipline
- Judging disputes between believers

**Instrumental**

None.

## Agent

Ministers and elders

## Norm

- 1 Tim 2:1-2
- Mt 14:3-4
- 1 Cor 6:1-8

## Boundary

Does not wield the sword or act as a political party.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Silence about God's law in public life.

**Usurpation:** Ecclesiocracy.
