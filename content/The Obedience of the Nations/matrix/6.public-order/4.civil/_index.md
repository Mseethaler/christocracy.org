**Civil in Public order**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P (Principal) | Justice as defined by God's law |

## End

Justice and public peace according to God's law.

## Means

**Direct**

- The sword
- Courts
- Restitution
- Just punishment
- Defense

**Instrumental**

None.

## Agent

Magistrates, judges and legislators

## Norm

- Rom 13:1-7
- Deut 16:18-20
- Deut 17:18-20
- Ex 18:21

## Boundary

Limited to punishing evil as God's law defines it: not savior, educator or provider.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Injustice; failure to punish evil.

**Usurpation:** The messianic state. Most usurpations elsewhere in the matrix originate here.

## Commentary

The fruit of the whole matrix: righteous magistrates come from discipled people.
