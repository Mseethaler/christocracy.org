**Self in Public order**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Self-government, citizenship, office |

## End

Self-government, faithful citizenship, and bearing office when called.

## Means

**Direct**

- Voting
- Jury service
- Seeking office
- Defense of the household

**Instrumental**

- Men's groups
- Local political organizations

## Agent

The citizen

## Norm

- Ex 18:21
- Prov 29:2
- Neh 4:14

## Boundary

The private man does not wield the sword of justice (Rom 12:19).

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Quietism; political apathy.

**Usurpation:** Vigilantism.
