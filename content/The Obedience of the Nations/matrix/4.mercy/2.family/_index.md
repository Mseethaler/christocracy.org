**Family in Mercy**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Care for your own first; hospitality |

## End

Care for one's own first (widows, aging parents, orphans in the family), and hospitality to others.

## Means

**Direct**

- Care for aging parents
- Kinship care
- Hospitality
- Adoption

**Instrumental**

- Mutual aid societies
- Adoption and foster ministries
- Pregnancy care centers
- Christian care homes

## Agent

Head of household

## Norm

- 1 Tim 5:4
- 1 Tim 5:8
- 1 Tim 5:16
- Rom 12:13
- Jas 1:27

## Boundary

Family care comes first; the church helps those with no family to help them.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Elderly handed to the state; no hospitality.

**Usurpation:** Insularity that ignores the household of faith.

## Commentary

Gifts are distinguished from contracts: mutual aid (gift) belongs here; risk pools (contract) belong in Economy.
