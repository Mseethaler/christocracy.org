**Self in Mercy**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Give from abundance |

## End

Love of neighbor by giving out of what one has.

## Means

**Direct**

- Alms
- Hospitality
- Personal aid

**Instrumental**

- Giving through mutual aid societies and ministries

## Agent

The believer

## Norm

- Eph 4:28
- 2 Cor 8:12
- Lk 10:30-37
- Mt 6:2-4
- Gal 6:10

## Boundary

Mercy does not subsidize sloth.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Stinginess.

**Usurpation:** Giving for show; indiscriminate giving that enables vice.
