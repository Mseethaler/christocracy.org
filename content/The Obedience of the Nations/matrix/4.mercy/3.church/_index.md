**Church in Mercy**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P (Principal) | The diaconate |

## End

Diaconal care for the household of faith, especially those without family to care for them.

## Means

**Direct**

- Deacons
- Benevolence collections
- Visitation

**Instrumental**

- Cooperative diaconal funds between congregations

## Agent

Deacons

## Norm

- Acts 6:1-6
- 1 Tim 5:3-16
- Gal 2:10

## Boundary

Supplements the family and does not replace it.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** No functioning diaconate; mercy outsourced to parachurch groups or government.

**Usurpation:** A diaconate that absorbs family duty; the social gospel replacing the Word.

## Commentary

Churches generally run their own diaconal funds, but some instruments for
specific purposes could be useful under certain conditions.
