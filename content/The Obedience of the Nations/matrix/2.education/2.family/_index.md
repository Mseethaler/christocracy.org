**Family in Education**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Parents hold the authority to educate |

## End

Raise children in the nurture and admonition of the Lord; hand down faith, wisdom and vocation.

## Means

**Direct**

- Home instruction
- Household catechesis
- Choosing and directing tutors and schools

**Instrumental**

- Christian schools
- Homeschool co-ops
- Tutors
- School networks
- Curriculum publishers

## Agent

Father and mother, the father as head

## Norm

- Deut 6:7
- Eph 6:4
- Prov 22:6
- Ps 78:4-7

## Boundary

Delegation is not abdication: parents may delegate instruction but not surrender authority.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Children handed to state schools; parents acting as customers of a school.

**Usurpation:** Refusing the church's catechetical role.

## Commentary

The decisive cell for degree 2. A school is the family's instrument, so its charter should name parents as principal.
