**Civil in Education**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| R (Regulative) | Protect parental liberty; promote true education |

## End

Protect the liberty of families to educate in truth; punish abuse and neglect as crimes God's law defines.

## Means

**Direct**

- Courts for crimes against children
- Tax breaks
- Promotion of righteous education

**Instrumental**

None.

## Agent

Magistrates

## Norm

- Rom 13:3-4
- Deut 6:7 (assigns education to parents)

## Boundary

No jurisdiction to fund, compel, license or set curriculum. Can restrict false
and idolatrous eduction. Promotes the true religion.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Failing to protect families' liberty.

**Usurpation:** State schooling, curriculum mandates and accreditation control.

## Commentary

The state has a legitimate interest in the education of the populous , as it is
necessary to the common good. The state ought to encourage and promote the
education of children by families in the truth, supporting a variety of
instruments for education through helpful laws, tax benefits, and more. The
state should uphold the education under the
first table of the Law, unto the true God, and not give any support to
idolatrous education. The state still though, needs respect the jurisdiction of
the parents first, and encourage parents in their calling.
