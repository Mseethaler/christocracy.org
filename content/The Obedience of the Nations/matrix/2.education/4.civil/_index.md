**Civil in Education**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| R (Regulative) | Protect parental liberty; promote true education |

## End

Protect the liberty of families to educate; punish abuse and neglect as crimes God's law defines.

## Means

**Direct**

- Courts for crimes against children

**Instrumental**

None.

## Agent

Magistrates

## Norm

- Rom 13:3-4
- Deut 6:7 (assigns education to parents)

## Boundary

No jurisdiction to fund, compel, license or set curriculum.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Failing to protect families' liberty.

**Usurpation:** State schooling, compulsory attendance, curriculum mandates and accreditation control.
