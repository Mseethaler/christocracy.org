**Church in Education**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+R (Principal + Regulative) | Catechize covenant children; equip fathers |

## End

Teach the whole counsel of God, catechize covenant children, and equip parents.

## Means

**Direct**

- Catechism instruction
- Preaching
- Training fathers to teach

**Instrumental**

- Hosting schools under parental principal (debated model)

## Agent

Ministers and elders

## Norm

- Mt 28:20
- Eph 4:11-12
- 2 Tim 2:2

## Boundary

Does not take over the family's authority to school its children.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** No catechesis; teaching left to schools and youth programs.

**Usurpation:** A session-governed school that displaces parents as principal.

## Commentary

Open question: parish schools run by a church have a long Reformed history. This map treats schools as family instruments; decide whether church-run schools are a legitimate alternative or a usurpation.
