**Self in Education**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Diligent learner |

## End

Growth in wisdom and knowledge in the fear of the Lord; teaching others where called.

## Means

**Direct**

- Study
- Submission to instruction
- Self-education across life

**Instrumental**

- Enrolling as student or apprentice
- Serving as teacher or tutor

## Agent

The learner; the teacher in his calling

## Norm

- Prov 1:7
- Prov 4:5-7
- 2 Tim 2:15

## Boundary

While a child, the learner is under parental authority.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Intellectual sloth; credentials without wisdom.

**Usurpation:** Child autonomy over parents' direction.
