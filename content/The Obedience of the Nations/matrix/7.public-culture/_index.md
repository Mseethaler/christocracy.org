Expressing and reproducing the faith and its fruits skillfully in public and commending them to foreign nations. The special responsibility of the nobility.

## Genus

| Government | Relation and summary |
|---|---|
| Self | P+C Make the true good and beautiful to glorify God |
| Family | P+C Household song, story and feast |
| Church | R Teach truth and beauty and goodness |
| Civil | R Encourage good works, punish libel, fraud and obscenity and blasphemy |
