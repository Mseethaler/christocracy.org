**Self in Public culture**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Make the true good and beautiful to glorify God |

## End

Create and steward beauty and truth through craft.

## Means

**Direct**

- Art
- Music
- Writing
- Performance

**Instrumental**

- Membership in publishers, labels, studios, arts guilds

## Agent

The artist and maker

## Norm

- Ex 31:1-6
- Ps 33:3
- Phil 4:8

## Boundary

Artistic liberty remains under God's law.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Consuming without making.

**Usurpation:** Art as autonomous self-expression; idolatry.
