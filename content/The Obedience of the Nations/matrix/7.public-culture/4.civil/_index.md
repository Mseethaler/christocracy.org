**Civil in Public culture**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| R (Regulative) | Punish libel, fraud and obscenity and blasphemy |

## End

Promotes and encourages the true good and beautiful. Punish libel, fraud and public obscenity as God's law defines them.

## Means

**Direct**

- Courts

**Instrumental**

None.

## Agent

Magistrates

## Norm

- Ex 20:16
- Ex 23:1

## Boundary

No state media, no subsidized art projects.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Public obscenity left unrestrained.

**Usurpation:** State propaganda; censorship of truth.
