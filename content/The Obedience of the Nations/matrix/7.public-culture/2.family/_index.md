**Family in Public culture**

| Relation | Summary |
|---|---|
| P+C (Principal + Constitutive) | Household song, story and feast |

## End

Build household culture (song, story, feast, tradition) and guard what enters the home.

## Means

**Direct**

- Household media choices
- Feasts and holy days
- Music and storytelling

**Instrumental**

- Publishers
- Media outlets
- Journalism
- Record labels
- Film studios
- Arts guilds

## Agent

Head of household

## Norm

- Ps 101:3
- Deut 16:14
- Ps 78:4

## Boundary

Patronage directs through ownership and support, not through control of the artist's conscience.

## Failure modes

**Neglect:** Screens as babysitter; culture outsourced to the entertainment industry.

**Usurpation:** Retreat from public culture into a ghetto.
